## About This Game

**Your lawn. Their war.**

THWACKALYPSE 64 is a retro low-poly bullet heaven about defending the most
sacred patch of grass in the suburbs. Pick a defender, walk into the yard,
and let your weapons do the talking — they fire themselves; your job is to
move, dash, and make build decisions that turn a spatula into a weather event.

**At 12:00, management arrives in person.** Survive the gnome hordes, the
flamingo migrations, and three escalating bosses, and you'll face
**KAREN, FINAL FORM** — a three-phase final boss who summons the HOA,
screams in Manager Voice, and teleports after you if you try to leave.
Beat her and the lawn is yours. Keep playing, and at 15:00 the audit begins.

### Features
- **7 defenders**, each with a signature dash — leave a burning grill trail,
  shoulder-check the crowd, or pickpocket enemies at 3× speed
- **10 weapons × 5 levels**, each with a unique **evolution**: BRUNCHNADO,
  MURDER HORNETS, DROP THE SUN, GNOME LEGION, CATEGORY 5…
- **5 bosses** including a recurring, effectively unkillable tax audit
- **4 maps × 2 difficulties**, unlocked by play — including a restless cemetery
- **📅 Daily Run**: date-seeded so the whole world fights the same waves;
  chase the daily thwack record
- **13 achievements**, a discovery almanac, permanent meta progression,
  level-up rerolls, and a revive for when Karen wins the argument
- **Full controller support** and Steam Deck-friendly out of the box
- Original synthesized soundtrack, zero asset packs, 100% legally distinct
